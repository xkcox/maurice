Case Study 1: The Complex Financial Simulation

The Challenge

Users face high anxiety and risk when learning to trade. The goal was to create a "sandbox" environment where complex financial maneuvers could be practiced without real-world loss.

Instructional Approach

Utilized Scenario-Based Learning. I mapped out "Happy Path" and "Error Path" branches to mirror real-life decision-making and risk assessment.

Development Details

Built complex variables and triggers in Articulate Storyline 360 to track user input, calculate positions, and provide real-time financial feedback.

Impact

Reduced user "time-to-proficiency" and lowered support calls regarding platform navigation significantly.

Case Study 2: Inclusive Design & Compliance

The Challenge

Creating mandatory provincial training that is 100% accessible to learners with visual, auditory, and motor impairments.

Instructional Approach

Followed Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles. Focused on clear information hierarchy and multi-modal content delivery.

Development Details

Optimized for JAWS/NVDA screen readers. Engineered custom tab-order logic to ensure a seamless "keyboard-only" experience.

Impact

Delivered gold-standard compliance modules used by thousands of organizations across Ontario to meet AODA requirements.
